# Portfolio rationale and application content

## The story for BETA

The strongest positioning is **a developer who turns human needs into connected, working systems**. Maia's design background helps explain the user problem; the case studies provide engineering evidence through APIs, persistence, device communication, and AI integration.

The [BETA role description](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/betatechnologiesinc/jobs/4465184009) emphasizes full-stack applications, internal tools, APIs, an AI-first approach, data products, and communicating technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders. Those are the reasons for the portfolio's project selection and structure. BETA branding is not copied, and the site does not imply employment or affiliation.

## What each case study includes

1. A short title and plain-language description.
2. The user problem and why it mattered.
3. Maia's own contribution, with AI assistance disclosed where documented.
4. Original screenshots, photos, or demo footage with captions.
5. The system's data/request flow and concrete technology choices.
6. A decision and its tradeoff.
7. Current limitations and a plausible next improvement.
8. Source/documentation links where a verified public source exists.

Do not add fictional user counts, speed improvements, test coverage, professional experience, AWS deployment, or aerospace claims. The current role asks for professional experience and AWS/serverless tooling; the supplied projects do not establish those qualifications. A concrete future example is more useful than an unsupported skills label.
